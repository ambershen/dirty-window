# Portfolio media and evidence plan

## Opening film — approximately 20–30 seconds

Show one simple gesture across all three materials. Include the hand and resulting surface response in at least part of the sequence. Capture real sound and leave enough time after release to show what the material does next. Avoid editing out tracking failures in footage presented as a technical demonstration.

## Three material clips

- Water: compare one finger with an open hand, then a sustained movement, a turn, and release. Record the gesture and its sound together; label planned mappings that are not implemented.
- Ice: a quick glide, slow retracing, then sustained contact or two-finger compression with sound. Identify pressure proxies in captions.
- Condensation: a short drawing, hand release, early local changes, and later disappearance. Use a clearly labeled time-lapse if accelerating it.

## Simulation studies — replace a generic iteration gallery

Prepare matched comparisons; change one parameter family at a time.

1. Gravity: constant droplet speed versus acceleration; pinning/release only when implemented.
2. Motion: identical gesture with short and long decay.
3. Texture: identical ice stroke at two accumulation strengths.
4. Sound: identical visual interaction with different audio mappings.
5. Tracking: show raw capture and output together; report measured delay only after measuring it.

Caption each comparison with: question / changed variable / observation / unresolved issue. Keep observations distinct from hypotheses.

Include archival Dirty Window footage of added water, dirtiness changes, and repeated swiping where available. Keep earlier experiments labeled separately from the current condensation build.

## Tool demonstration — future capture

Show record gesture → replay → adjust behavior → save recipe. Do not use a conceptual editor mockup as evidence of a working tool; label mockups explicitly.

## Wall projection — ongoing experiment

Capture the full setup: Mac, projector, camera, wall, and participant. Test alignment, shadows, reach, and whether a person understands where interaction occurs. Record camera placement and lighting. Separate projection output from the sensing method.

## Suggested asset naming

`water-motion-01.mp4`, `ice-friction-01.mp4`, `condensation-recovery-01.mp4`, `gravity-comparison-01.mp4`, `projection-setup-01.jpg`.

Keep raw captures, edited clips, captions, and source/license notes together when collecting media. No new media is included in this package yet.
