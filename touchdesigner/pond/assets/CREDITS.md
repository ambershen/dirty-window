# Water audio

hand-in-water.mp3 is the high-quality MP3 preview of **Water, Gentle Movement.wav** by **Daen23** (June 2, 2018).

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Daen23/sounds/431627/
- Recording description: Gentle movement of a hand in water.
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/431/431627_2363418-hq.mp3

The app plays short excerpts at original speed and pitch, with volume envelopes and subtle stereo positioning. No generated audio is mixed in.

## Outdoor pond revision (current playback)

- `pond-entry.mp3`: **Splash, small #2**, Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank. Small stone thrown into a pond, recorded outdoors. CC0. https://bigsoundbank.com/splash-small-2-s1530.html
  Download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/1530.mp3
- `lake-movement.mp3`: **Water Splash in Lake 01.wav**, vero.marengere (2020), CC0. Outdoor lake splashing, Zoom H4N Pro with the author's noise removal. https://freesound.org/people/vero.marengere/sounds/524511/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/524/524511_6605732-hq.mp3

These are outdoor-water stand-ins, not recordings of a fingertip entering a pond. Entry uses a quiet pond impact; release uses its settling tail; motion uses low-level lake excerpts. Playback stays at original speed and pitch, with fades and subtle panning. The earlier bathtub recording is no longer used.
