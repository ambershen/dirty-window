# Expand and tag the sand sketch library

Pieces are SVG polylines in a 100 × 100 box. They are generated by `scripts/sketch-art.mjs` from entries in `scripts/sketch-art.json`, and source photos live in `scripts/art-sources/`. Read the header comment of `sketch-art.mjs` and two existing motif entries before adding any.

## Adding a piece
1. **Find a source.** Check in this order:
   - the `needs` lists in `src/vj/stories/*.json`;
   - a public-domain image of an artwork with a clear silhouette on a plain ground. Try Met Open Access (`collectionapi.metmuseum.org`, `isPublicDomain`), the Art Institute of Chicago API (`is_public_domain`), and Wikimedia Commons. Prefer vase paintings, woodblocks, reliefs and silhouettes over photos of sculpture in a room.
   - If nothing traces cleanly, generate a reference with Fish Audio `generate_image`. Use a prompt like: *"single-weight black line drawing of <subject>, full figure in profile facing right, white background, no shading, no texture, in the style of <tone from the story>"*. Always run `estimate_image_generation` first and get the user's approval for the credits.
   - **Paintings** (colour, modelling, busy backgrounds) almost never trace cleanly from a photograph. Of 17 tried on 2026-10-22, 3 survived.
     - Convert them first with Fish Audio `generate_image_edit`, using the photo as `ref`. Prompt: *"single-weight bold black outlines on pure white, exact composition and contours, only silhouette and main inner lines, no shading or texture"*. Then trace the result with `sil: dark, silBlur: 3, threshold: 200`.
     - Sculpture on a plain backdrop, flat-colour prints and drawings trace directly.
     - Pieces waiting for conversion are listed in `scripts/sketch-art-pending.json`.
     - The free Fish plan allows 3 images a day.
2. Save the image to `scripts/art-sources/<id>.jpg`. Add an entry with `set: "motif"`, `story`, `role`, `faces`, `source`, `license` and `author`, then run `node scripts/sketch-art.mjs`. Add credits to `src/vj/ART-CREDITS.md`.
3. **Look at it.** Run `node scripts/render-scene.mjs --piece <id>` and check the PNG. Tune `crop`, `low`/`high`, `blur` and `budget` until the silhouette is one clear outline, with at most 3 tries. If it still fails, drop the piece and say why.

## Tagging (`/sand-library tag`)
Render pieces in groups of 8 with `--piece a,b,…` and look at each one. Then write or merge into `src/vj/motifCatalog.json`:
```json
[{ "id": "ship", "depicts": "Greek ship, oars out, sail up, facing right", "quality": 3,
   "weight": "heavy | medium | light", "anchor": "ground | water | sky", "notes": "stern is noisy" }]
```
- **Quality scale:** 5 is clean and instantly readable; 3 is readable with noise; 1 is a blob.
- The composer sees `depicts` and `quality` through `node scripts/catalog.mjs`. Pieces scoring below 3 should be redone or left alone.
