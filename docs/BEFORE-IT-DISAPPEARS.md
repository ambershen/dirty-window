# Before It Disappears

Phone-first fogged-glass study: `?mode=fog`.

Slow strokes clear narrow writing lines; fast swipes broaden the cleared path. Each segment stays clear for four seconds and returns to fog over eight seconds, independently of later strokes. Stationary contact keeps its small patch clear. Wet beads form along moving strokes and slide downward after a short delay, leaving fine trails that fade separately. The surface resets on viewport resize.

The initial view is a locally drawn abstract night scene. The photo button replaces it with a user-selected image processed locally. The reset button clears all writing and droplets. No camera or microphone is requested; this study is intentionally silent. The title fades after the first touch.

Existing water, ice and projector modes are unchanged. This is a visual prototype, not a condensation physics solver. Phone feel and performance need real-device testing.
