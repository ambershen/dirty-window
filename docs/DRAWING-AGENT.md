# Drawing agent for the sand scroll

Status: design, not built. The motif library and storylines below exist; the agent itself does not yet.

## Why

On the VJ deck, sand is an endless scroll (`src/vj/sandDeck.ts`): the sand travels left with the tempo, works are drawn as they come in on the right, and the wind wears them away on the left. The drawing is played by the music, but what is drawn is a random famous work with a random building beside it. Each scene stands alone, so the scroll does not read as one continuous picture, and the hand-drawn look can feel scribbly. The goal is a scroll that reads like a mural or a handscroll: one ground, one story, one way of drawing, carried from scene to scene.

## Division of work

- **Rhythm (code and parameters).** When a stroke lands, how fast the fingertip moves, how hard it presses, the scroll's speed, drops and breakdowns. This stays as it is.
- **Content (the agent).** What is drawn, and how scenes connect. The agent decides for itself. We give it more to combine (motifs, storylines, rules of drawing), not a fixed route.

## What the agent is given

- **Motifs** (`src/vj/motifSketches.ts`, generated by `scripts/sketch-art.mjs` from entries with `set: "motif"`): figures, creatures, ships and scenery, each a sand line sketch in a 100 × 100 box, tagged with its storylines (`story`), its `role` (character, creature, vehicle, scenery, sky, scene) and optionally which way it faces and whether it stands on the ground. Preview: `/?mode=sketches` (dev only), sections by storyline.
  - Drawn by hand in the script: the sea (Greek wave scroll), olive tree, auspicious clouds, mountains, pine, moon, sun.
  - Traced from Wikimedia Commons (credits in `src/vj/ART-CREDITS.md`): ship (Exekias), Odysseus under the ram (Delphi bronze), a hero (Exekias), Athena, the blinding of the Cyclops; Sun Wukong (Yoshitoshi), Xuanzang (Kamakura painting), the white dragon horse (the Flying Horse of Gansu), a Tang horse.
  - Tracing quality varies. Clean: the hand-drawn pieces and the dragon horse. Readable: ship, ram, hero, Tang horse. Weak: Wukong (a blob), Xuanzang (rough), Athena, the Cyclops scene. Dropped: the Sirens, Penelope, the Mykonos Trojan horse, a Zhu Bajie lantern, Fan Kuan's mountain, Mori Sosen's monkey (low contrast or figure merged with background).
- **Storylines** (`src/vj/storylines.ts`): the Odyssey (10 episodes) and Journey to the West (9 episodes). Each storyline names its tone and ground (sea, land or both); each episode has a scene description, cast, places and a mood (quiet, journey, busy, climax). Not a script: the agent chooses which episodes to tell, in what order, and how.
- **Rules of drawing.** Sand lines are grooves with raised rims, so: one continuous silhouette, then a few strong inner lines; figures in profile; one scale for figures across the scroll; the ground line, river or sea continuous across scene edges.

## What the agent does (proposed)

Works scene by scene along the scroll. Before each scene it sees how the previous one ends (a short text summary and a small rendered image). Tools:

- look at the scroll so far;
- place a motif (position along the scroll, ground height, scale, mirrored or not);
- draw its own strokes (SVG path data in scroll coordinates), not limited to the library;
- continue the ground, river or sea line;
- finish the scene, tagging its mood and the episode it tells.

It may also draw new motifs itself from a reference image and the rules above (for example "Wukong raising his staff", "the monk on the horse"), which may give cleaner, more consistent pieces than tracing photographs.

## How the music uses it

The output is a scroll of scenes, each tagged with a mood. On the deck, the music picks the next scene by section: a breakdown takes a quiet one, a drop a climax, otherwise the next in the scroll. Content comes from the agent, order from the music, every stroke's timing and pressure from the rhythm.

## Running it

- Offline first: a Node script using the Claude API with tool use, generating a whole scroll (for example 20–40 scenes) into a data file, previewed on `/?mode=sketches` before it reaches the deck. Each run gives a different scroll.
- Live later, if wanted: the same agent generating ahead of the music during a set, with the offline scroll as a fallback when the network is unreliable.
- Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (kept in `.env.local`, never committed).

## Open questions

- How much the agent should invent versus combine library motifs.
- Whether scenes should be drawn as lines only, or partly pressed as relief (flat-bottomed moulds, as the buildings are) for a more mural-like surface.
- How the deck should stage a scene: the order the agent intends, or an order derived from the rhythm.
